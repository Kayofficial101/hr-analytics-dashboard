## Business Problem
HR department wanted to understand why employees leave. Analyzed 1,400+ employee records to find attrition patterns and build a predictive risk profile.

## Tools
- **Excel**: Pivot tables, slicers, interactive dashboard
- **SQL**: Data preparation and segmentation
- **Python**: Statistical analysis and correlation heatmaps

## Key Findings
- Attrition rate: 16.1% overall
- Employees working overtime are 3.1x more likely to leave
- Single employees under 30 have 2x higher attrition than married employees
- Lab Technicians and Sales Reps have the highest attrition (>20%)
- Employees with <2 years at company account for 59% of all attrition

## Recommendations
- Cap overtime at 10hrs/week for high-risk departments
- Implement structured mentorship for first 2 years
- Review compensation for Lab Technicians vs market benchmarks
- Quarterly stay interviews for employees flagged as flight risks
